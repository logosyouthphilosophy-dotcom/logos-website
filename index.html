Our Mission

Philosophy for everyone

Logos is a youth-led philosophy organisation working to make philosophy accessible. Most students never encounter it at school, and certainly not in the years when it would do them the most good.

It matters twice over. It sharpens how you think and argue, and it shapes the moral and ethical compass you end up carrying.

So we run three things: a podcast that unpacks difficult ideas with professors and people working in the field, a student-led journal, and a set of introductory resources for anyone starting from nothing.

A writing workshop with Oxford faculty

We are running a workshop on how philosophical writing actually works, led by Oxford faculty. It covers building an argument, handling objections and citing properly, and is aimed at anyone who has never written a philosophy essay before.

Places are limited, and students who register their interest get first refusal. Dates will be announced by email to everyone on the register.
