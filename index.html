Most students never meet philosophy at school

Logos is a philosophy organisation run by secondary school students. We started it in Dubai because philosophy is usually locked behind a university door, and because it gets treated as a hobby rather than a real skill.

It matters twice over. It sharpens how you think and argue, and it shapes the moral compass you end up carrying.

Everything we run is free, and all of it is open to students anywhere in the world.

What we run

All of it free, all of it open to students anywhere.

The journal and essay competition

Students publish their own argument on a question they cannot let go of. Our first issue is an open competition with no set theme, closing 15 December 2026. Essays are written and published in English.
